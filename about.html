About Halden

Specialists in building documentation, compliance processing, and council approval support for commercial property developers.

Building Compliance, Made Simple.

Halden is a building documentation and compliance consultancy based in Johannesburg, South Africa. We specialise in transforming complex existing building conditions into reliable, submission-ready information for property developers and commercial property owners.

Our team of architectural practitioners brings together expertise in measured surveys, architectural documentation, and compliance processing to provide accurate building records that enable confident decision-making.

Our Values

  • Accuracy: We deliver precise, reliable building information.
  • Confidence: We help you make informed property decisions.
  • Efficiency: We reduce project delays and duplicated work.
  • Clarity: We simplify complex compliance requirements.
